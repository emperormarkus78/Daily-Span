How to play

  1. Every answer is a number — a year, a weight, a count.
  2. Don't guess the number. Use the sliders to lay a range on the line: where you think it is, and how wide a net you need. A narrow net is worth more than a wide one.
  3. Then commit. HEDGE is safe — you score if you're right and lose nothing if you're wrong. CALL pays 1.75× but a miss costs you half. CALLED SHOT pays triple, and a miss costs you the lot.
  4. Five questions. A perfect day is 500. A bad one can go below zero.
  5. Over time the game tracks how often you're actually right at each level, and tells you whether you're overconfident. Most people are.

The trick isn't knowing more than everyone else. It's knowing how much you know.
